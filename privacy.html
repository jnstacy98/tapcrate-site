Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

SummaryResDemandOur CashflowThis websiteYour choicesContact

This policy explains what information TapCrate ("we", "us") collects through our apps and this website, how it's used, and the choices you have. It covers ResDemand and tapcrate.dev. Our Cashflow has its own privacy policy.

Summary

  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • ResDemand keeps everything on your iPhone. We don't collect any of it.
  • Our Cashflow stores your budget in the cloud so it syncs between devices and the people you share it with. Its own privacy policy explains the details.

ResDemand

ResDemand works without an account and without an internet connection. The projects, load calculations and settings you enter are stored only on your device. We don't receive them, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

When you export a PDF report, it's created on your device. Where it goes after that is up to you: if you email it, message it or save it to Files, it's handled by the app or service you choose.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple shares only limited sales information with us, such as the number of downloads.

If you delete ResDemand, your projects are deleted with it, unless they're included in your own device backup.

Our Cashflow

Our Cashflow, on iPhone and the web, has its own privacy policy at ourcashflow.app/privacy. That policy is the one that applies to the app: what it collects, bank connections through Plaid, the optional Claude connection, who it's shared with, how long it's kept, and how to delete it.

This website

tapcrate.dev doesn't use cookies or advertising. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views and measures how quickly pages load without cookies and without tracking you across other sites. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, also processes basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Your choices and rights

  • For Our Cashflow, see the choices in its privacy policy, including how to export your data and delete your account.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we'll honor those requests.

Children

Our apps aren't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we'll let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: ourcashflow@tapcrate.dev
