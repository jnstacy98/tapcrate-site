Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

SummaryResDemandOur CashflowThis websiteYour choicesContact

This policy explains what information TapCrate ("we", "us") collects through our apps and this website, how it's used, and the choices you have. It covers ResDemand, Our Cashflow (on iPhone and the web), and tapcrate.dev.

Summary

  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • ResDemand keeps everything on your iPhone. We don't collect any of it.
  • Our Cashflow stores your budget in the cloud so it syncs between devices and the people you share it with. Bank linking is optional and handled by Plaid.
  • You can delete your Our Cashflow account and its data from inside the app at any time.

ResDemand

ResDemand works without an account and without an internet connection. The projects, load calculations and settings you enter are stored only on your device. We don't receive them, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

When you export a PDF report, it's created on your device. Where it goes after that is up to you: if you email it, message it or save it to Files, it's handled by the app or service you choose.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. Apple shares only limited sales information with us, such as the number of downloads.

If you delete ResDemand, your projects are deleted with it, unless they're included in your own device backup.

Our Cashflow

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a password if you sign up with email. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email (or Apple's private relay address) from that service.
  • Budget information: accounts, balances, transactions, bills, income, recurring items, debts, categories and notes that you enter or import.
  • Receipt photos: pictures you take or choose to attach to a transaction.
  • Household members: when you invite someone to share a budget, we use their email address to send and match the invite. Everyone in a shared budget can see and edit its information, and members can see each other's names and email addresses.

Bank connections (optional)

If you choose to connect a bank account, Our Cashflow uses Plaid Inc. to do it. You sign in to your bank through Plaid; we never see or store your bank username or password. Plaid sends us account names and types, balances and transactions for the accounts you choose, and we store them with your budget. Plaid's handling of your information is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy. You can disconnect a bank at any time in the app, and deleting your account removes your bank connections at Plaid.

Information collected automatically

  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send bill reminders and budget alerts.
  • Account identifiers: a user ID assigned when you sign up, used to keep your data separate and secure.
  • Location stays on your iPhone: if you allow location access, the app suggests the payee you usually pay where you are. Those places are saved only on your iPhone and are never uploaded. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

Our Cashflow does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services, and does not track you.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, store and sync your budget across your devices and household, import bank transactions you've connected, attach receipts, send the reminders you've turned on, and answer your support requests. We don't use your financial information for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

Connecting Our Cashflow to Claude (optional)

You can choose to connect your budget to Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, so Claude can read it while helping you plan. This is off unless you set it up and approve it. The connection is read-only, and Claude can see only the budget you approved. While it's connected, the information Claude reads is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy. You can remove the connection at any time from Claude or by deleting your Our Cashflow account.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that run parts of the app for us, and only so they can provide that service:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, cloud database, file storage for receipts, server functions and push notifications. Your data is stored on Google Cloud servers.
  • Plaid: bank connections, if you use them.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, notification delivery and App Store purchases.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others. If TapCrate is ever sold or merged, this information may transfer to the new owner under the same commitments as this policy.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account. Deleting your account removes your profile, sign-in, receipt photos, bank connections and any budget you're the only member of. A budget you share with others stays available to them, with you removed. Deleted information may remain for a short time in our service providers' systems before it's permanently erased.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's infrastructure. Access is limited to you and the members of budgets you share, and bank access tokens are kept on our servers rather than on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your devices locked.

This website

tapcrate.dev doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Your choices and rights

  • View, change or export your Our Cashflow data in the app.
  • Disconnect banks, turn off notifications or location, and remove the Claude connection whenever you like.
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us to do it by email.
  • Ask us what information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act, and we'll honor those requests.

Children

Our apps aren't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we'll let you know in the app or by email before it takes effect.
