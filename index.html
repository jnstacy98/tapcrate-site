ResDemand

Available on the App Store for iPhone

Load calculations under the 2023 NEC for dwellings, multifamily and commercial buildings, plus the field calculators an electrician reaches for every day.

  • Dwelling load calcs with the Standard and Optional methods, compared side by side
  • Multifamily, commercial and existing-service calculations
  • Generator sizing
  • Voltage drop, wire sizing, box fill, conduit bending, transformer and motor circuits
  • Panel schedules and permit-ready PDF reports showing each step
  • Projects saved on your iPhone, with no account needed

Our Cashflow

Coming soon to the App Store · now in TestFlight beta

A shared cash flow planner for households: see what's coming in, what's due, and what's safe to spend before the next paycheck.

  • One budget shared with your partner or household
  • Bills, paychecks and recurring items on a calendar
  • Category budgets, debts and account balances in one place
  • Optional bank sync through Plaid
  • Receipt photos kept with each transaction
  • Home screen widgets and bill reminders

About TapCrate

TapCrate is owned and run by Jack Stacy, an electrician by trade who builds software on the side. ResDemand is built for the calculations electricians do in the field and at the permit counter. Our Cashflow is built for planning a household budget around real paydays and real due dates.

No ads, no selling of data, and no tracking across other apps or websites. If something doesn't work the way it should, you'll hear back from the person who wrote it.
