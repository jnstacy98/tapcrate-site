Support

Help for ResDemand and Our Cashflow

Email ourcashflow@tapcrate.dev with the app name, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps. You'll usually hear back within two business days.

ResDemand

Which code edition does ResDemand use?

Calculations follow the 2023 National Electrical Code, and each report shows the edition it was calculated with. Always check whether your jurisdiction has adopted that edition or made local amendments.

How do I send a report to an inspector or customer?

Open a project's result and choose Share PDF. You can email it, message it, print it or save it to Files.

Will my projects move to a new iPhone?

Projects are stored on your iPhone, not in an online account. They move with an iCloud or computer backup when you restore it to a new phone. If you delete the app, its projects are deleted too.

I bought ResDemand. How do I install it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Purchased, and download ResDemand again at no charge.

I think a result is wrong.

Email us the project's PDF report and the section of the code you're comparing against. Every report shows each step of the calculation, which makes it quick to track down.

Our Cashflow

How do I join the beta?

Our Cashflow is in TestFlight while we finish it for the App Store. Email us to ask for an invite, and install Apple's TestFlight app on your iPhone.

How do I share a budget with my partner?

In the app, go to your household and choose Invite Someone, then send them the invite. Invite codes work once and expire after a few days, so send a new one if it has run out.

Is connecting my bank safe?

Bank connections go through Plaid, which many finance apps use. You sign in to your bank through Plaid, and Our Cashflow never sees your bank password. You can disconnect a bank in the app whenever you like. See the Privacy Policy for details.

My bank transactions look out of date.

Banks often take a day or two to post transactions, and pending ones may change. If a connection stops updating for longer than that, your bank may need you to sign in again. Reconnect it from the app, and email us if that doesn't fix it.

How do I export my data?

In Settings, use Export Transactions or the bills and income export to save a spreadsheet file (CSV).

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This removes your account, receipts, bank connections and any budget only you belong to. Budgets shared with others stay with them. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.
