Support

Help for ResDemand and Our Cashflow

Email ourcashflow@tapcrate.dev with the app name, your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps. You'll usually hear back within two business days.

ResDemand

Which code edition does ResDemand use?

Calculations follow the 2023 National Electrical Code, and each report shows the edition it was calculated with. Always check whether your jurisdiction has adopted that edition or made local amendments.

How do I send a report to an inspector or customer?

Open a project's result and choose Share PDF. You can email it, message it, print it or save it to Files.

Will my projects move to a new iPhone?

Projects are stored on your iPhone, not in an online account. They move with an iCloud or computer backup when you restore it to a new phone. If you delete the app, its projects are deleted too.

I bought ResDemand. How do I install it on another device?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the App Store, tap your profile picture, then Purchased, and download ResDemand again at no charge.

I think a result is wrong.

Email us the project's PDF report and the section of the code you're comparing against. Every report shows each step of the calculation, which makes it quick to track down.

Our Cashflow

Help for Our Cashflow, including bank connections, sharing a budget, exporting data and deleting your account, is at ourcashflow.app/support.

How do I join the beta?

Our Cashflow is in TestFlight while we finish it for the App Store. Email us to ask for an invite, and install Apple's TestFlight app on your iPhone. You can also use it on the web at ourcashflow.app.
